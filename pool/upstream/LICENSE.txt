Local changes (2026-11) are listed at the top of renderer.js: water tint,
host-supplied tile texture, host-supplied caustic texture size, and a reduced
caustic highlight strength. The original tile photo and sky cubemap JPGs from
the upstream demo are NOT used; the pool study generates its own tile and sky
textures at runtime, so no third-party image rights are involved.
